# QllmDocs

**AI-powered document management, built for individuals and teams.**

QllmDocs is a cloud-based document management platform that combines AI-powered search, secure access controls, and effortless organization — helping businesses store, find, and manage their documents faster and smarter.

## 📖 Overview

Traditional document storage tools are built for humans to upload and download files — not to help them actually *find* what they need. QllmDocs is designed to close that gap: an AI layer sits on top of secure cloud storage, so search feels less like digging through folders and more like asking a question.

It's built to serve everyone from individual freelancers organizing their own files to teams that need shared, permission-controlled document libraries.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔍 **AI-Powered Search** — Find documents by meaning and content, not just by filename.
- ☁️ **Cloud-Based Storage** — Access your documents securely from anywhere.
- 🔐 **Secure Access & Role-Based Control** — Control who can view, edit, or manage each document.
- 🛡️ **AES-256 Encryption** — Enterprise-grade encryption to keep files protected at rest.
- 👥 **Built for Individuals & Teams** — Scales from solo use to organization-wide document management.
- 🆓 **Free 90-Day Trial (Monthly Basic Plan)** — Full access with no credit card required.
